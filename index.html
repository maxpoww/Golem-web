The ground

The OS that cannot rot.

Golem stands on NixOS: the whole system is intent written to a file. Installs are declarative. Updates are atomic — they either happen completely or not at all. And every state your machine has ever been in is a generation you can return to.

An update can never break your computer. Not "rarely." Never. If anything is not to your liking, one click takes you back to yesterday's machine. No mainstream OS can say that. Try it — break the one on the right.

Principles

What we refuse to build.

Ads inside your operating system.
Telemetry. Your machine reports to no one.
Upsells dressed as features.
Configuration as a hobby.
Terminals as a requirement.
Interruptions dressed as help.
Updates that can break your machine.

Every one of these exists elsewhere because it makes someone money or saves someone effort. We're not that someone.
